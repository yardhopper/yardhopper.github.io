Yard Hopper

Jobs, recurring visits, invoicing, photos, and equipment maintenance for lawn-care businesses — all in one app.

Yard Hopper helps small lawn-care and landscaping businesses schedule recurring visits, log work and expenses, keep equipment maintenance on schedule, manage customers, and send invoices — with optional cloud sync across your devices.

What's included

  • Recurring visit scheduling — set a pattern once (weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly) and get a week-by-week calendar of upcoming visits. Log, move, or skip a single visit without touching the pattern; order a busy day's stops by route.
  • Before & after photos — attach proof-of-work photos to a job, automatically embedded in the invoice PDF you send the customer.
  • Receipt photos on expenses — snap a photo of a receipt when you log an expense, so it's attached to the record instead of lost in your camera roll.
  • Keep customers in the loop — optionally send a quick email or text update with a photo when a job's done, and text an invoice straight to a customer who's easier to reach by phone than email.
  • Invoicing — build invoices from unbilled work or recurring templates, then print, export, email, or text them.
  • Equipment & maintenance — track blade and engine hours automatically as you log jobs, with reminders before service is due.
  • Reports (Pro) — income, expenses, and net balance over any date range, ready to print or export.
  • Works offline first — your data lives on your device, so you can log jobs anywhere, even without a signal.
  • Cloud sync (Pro) — share your data across your own devices, no account required.
  • Available in 15 languages.

Free to start

Yard Hopper is free to use with up to 5 jobs, 5 customers, 25 invoices, and 1 recurring visit. Yard Hopper Pro removes those limits and adds full reports and cloud sync across your devices.
