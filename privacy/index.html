Yard Hopper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Yard Hopper is built for small lawn-care and landscaping businesses to track jobs, expenses, equipment, customers, and invoices. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

Yard Hopper stores the information you enter while using the app, including:

  • Job records — dates, activities, hours, location (only if you use "Use current location," which asks for permission each time), and notes
  • Expenses and equipment maintenance records
  • Customer details you add — name, address, email, and/or phone number, if you choose to enter them
  • Invoices and recurring invoice templates
  • Your business settings — such as business name, owner name, phone, and email, if you fill these in for use on printed documents

We don't require you to create an account, and we don't collect advertising identifiers, run analytics, or use tracking SDKs of any kind.

Where your data is stored

By default, all of your data stays on your device, in local app storage.

If you turn on Cloud sync (Settings → Cloud sync), your data is also stored with our sync provider, Supabase, so it can be shared across your own devices. Cloud sync uses an anonymous, randomly generated "household" identifier and a short join code — it is not tied to your name, email, or any personal login.

Cloud sync is entirely optional. If you never turn it on, nothing you enter leaves your device except when you explicitly export a backup file, print, email, or export an invoice/report yourself.

Notifications

Yard Hopper can schedule local reminders on your own device — for example, for upcoming recurring invoices. These are scheduled entirely on-device; no notification data is sent to or through our servers.

Your customers' information

If you enter details about your own customers (name, address, email, phone), that information is handled the same way as the rest of your data: stored locally, and optionally synced if you enable Cloud sync. As the business using Yard Hopper, you're responsible for having the appropriate basis to store and process your customers' information under applicable law.

Third parties

We use the following services to run the app:

  • Supabase — stores your data if you enable Cloud sync
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — app distribution

We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers, and we don't sell your data.

Your choices

  • Export a full backup of your data at any time from Settings → Backup
  • Turn Cloud sync on or off at any time in Settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored locally on that device
  • To request deletion of data stored via Cloud sync, email us at the address below

Children's privacy

Yard Hopper is a business tool intended for adults running a lawn-care or landscaping business. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
