Frequently asked questions

How do I sync my data across devices?

Go to Settings → Cloud sync → "Set up sync on this device." This gives you a short code. On your other device, open Settings → Cloud sync and enter that code under "Join an existing household by code." Both devices will then share the same jobs, customers, expenses, equipment, and invoices.

Where can I find my sync code again?

On any device that's already linked, open Settings → Cloud sync — the code is shown there so you can share it with another device at any time.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Go to Settings → Backup → "Export data" to save a backup file you can store or share. Use "Import data" on the same or a different device to restore from a backup file. Importing replaces everything currently on that device.

Can I log more than one activity for the same visit?

Yes. On New Job, the Activities field lets you select several activities (mowing, fertilizing, hedge trimming, and more), or type in a custom one — all billed together as a single visit with one hours/rate total.

How do I take a piece of equipment out of service?

On the Equipment & Maintenance screen, tap "Mark unavailable" on that equipment (optionally add a reason). Unavailable equipment won't show up when logging a new job until you mark it available again.

How do I change the app's language?

Go to Settings → Language and pick from the list, or choose "System default" to follow your device's language automatically.

How do I create and send an invoice?

Go to Invoices, pick a customer, then select their unbilled jobs (or add a line item manually). Tap "Create invoice," then use Email, Print, or Export PDF to send it.

Is my data private?

By default, everything stays on your device. If you turn on Cloud sync, your data is also stored with our sync provider so your devices can share it. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
