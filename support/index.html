Frequently asked questions

How do I set my default hourly rate and currency?

Go to Settings and set your default hourly rate and preferred currency. These pre-fill every new job automatically, saving time in the field.

How do I change the app's language?

Go to Settings → Language and pick from the list, or choose "System default" to follow your device's language automatically.

How do I add a new customer?

Open New Job, tap the customer field, and choose "Add New Customer." You only need to do this once — they'll appear in every future job. For fuller details (address, email, phone, default rate) manage customers from the Invoices tab.

How do I add a piece of equipment?

Go to the Equipment & Maintenance tab and tap +. Add your ride-on mowers, push mowers, string trimmers, hedge trimmers, leaf blowers, and other tools. The Yard Hopper tracks blade hours, oil change intervals, and engine hours, alerting you when service is due.

How do I track blade sharpening and oil change intervals?

When adding or editing equipment, set the service intervals (blade hours, oil change hours). After each service, log a Blade Sharpening or Oil Change event to reset the counters automatically.

How do I log a lawn care job?

Tap New Job, select the customer and date, choose activities (mowing, trimming, edging, pruning, etc.), enter hours, and optionally add before/after photos. Tap Save — the job is available for invoicing immediately.

How do I set up recurring visits?

After creating a job, tap Make Recurring to set weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly visit schedules. The app will pre-populate your calendar and remind you on the day.

How do I generate and send a PDF invoice?

Open the Invoices tab, create or find the invoice, then tap Export PDF. Share it directly via email, AirDrop, or any app on your device.

How do I optimise my daily route?

In the Route tab, add your stops for the day and tap Optimise Route to reorder by shortest drive time. Each stop opens Maps navigation with one tap.

What does The Yard Hopper Pro include?

Pro unlocks unlimited jobs and customers, PDF invoice generation, route optimisation, equipment maintenance tracking with automatic hour resets, photo attachments, expense tracking, profit & loss reports, and Household Cloud Sync across devices.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Is my data backed up?

All data is stored locally on your device (offline-first). Enable Household Cloud Sync (Pro feature) to sync across multiple devices. We also recommend enabling iCloud backup on your iPhone so data is included in Apple's standard device backup.
