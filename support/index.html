Frequently asked questions

How do I set my default hourly rate and currency?

Go to Settings and set your default hourly rate and preferred currency. These pre-fill every new job automatically, saving you time in the field.

How do I change the app's language?

Go to Settings → Language and pick from the list, or choose "System default" to follow your device's language automatically.

How do I add a new customer?

Open New Job, tap the customer field, and choose "Add New Customer." You only need to do this once — they'll show up every time after that. For fuller customer details (address, email, phone, default rate), manage customers from the Invoices tab.

How do I add a piece of equipment?

Go to the Equipment & Maintenance tab and add each piece of equipment you own — mower, trimmer, blower, and so on. Yard Hopper automatically tracks blade and engine hours as you log jobs, and reminds you when service is due.

How do I take a piece of equipment out of service?

On the Equipment & Maintenance screen, tap "Mark unavailable" on that equipment (optionally add a reason). Unavailable equipment won't show up when logging a new job until you mark it available again.

How do I log a job?

Open New Job, pick your customer, choose the date and activity type, enter your hours, and optionally capture your location with one tap. Select any equipment used and hit Save.

Can I log more than one activity for the same visit?

Yes. On New Job, the Activities field lets you select several activities (mowing, fertilizing, hedge trimming, and more), or type in a custom one — all billed together as a single visit with one hours/rate total.

Can I attach photos to a job or expense?

Yes. On New Job you can attach before/after photos, which are automatically included as proof of work on that job's invoice PDF. On New Expense you can attach a photo of the receipt. Photos are stored only on your device — they're not included in Cloud sync or in an exported backup. You can see how much space they're using, or clear them all, from Settings → Backup.

How do I track an expense?

Open New Expense, pick a category, enter the amount, and optionally link it to a specific piece of equipment so your reports stay accurate.

How do recurring visits work?

Open the Schedule tab and create a Job Template for a customer — pick a frequency (weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly) and Yard Hopper keeps a calendar of upcoming visits for you. Tap any visit to log it as a real job, move it to a different date, skip it just once, or edit the template to change the pattern going forward. On a busy day, turn on "Route order" to sort that day's visits by distance from each other. The free plan includes 1 recurring visit; Yard Hopper Pro unlocks unlimited recurring visits.

How do I create and send an invoice?

Go to Invoices, pick a customer, then select their unbilled jobs (or add a line item manually). Tap "Create invoice," then use Email, Print, or Export PDF to send it.

How are recurring invoices different from recurring visits?

Recurring visits (Schedule tab) generate upcoming jobs to do. Recurring invoices (Invoices tab) are a separate, optional pattern with their own saved line items and frequency — when one is due, it appears under "Due templates" with a Generate button. If you log a scheduled visit for a customer who also has an active recurring invoice template, Yard Hopper will offer to add that job onto the next invoice.

How do I run a report?

Go to Reports, select a date range, and see your income, expenses, and net balance at a glance. Tap Print or Export PDF to save or share your records. Reports is available on Yard Hopper Pro.

How do I back up or restore my data?

Go to Settings → Backup → "Export data" to save a backup file you can store or share. Use "Import data" on the same or a different device to restore from a backup file. Importing replaces everything currently on that device.

How do I sync my data across devices?

Go to Settings → Cloud sync → "Set up sync on this device." This gives you a short code. On your other device, open Settings → Cloud sync and enter that code under "Join an existing household by code." Both devices will then share the same jobs, customers, expenses, equipment, and invoices. If a device ever falls out of sync, Settings will offer to relink it.

Where can I find my sync code again?

On any device that's already linked, open Settings → Cloud sync — the code is shown there so you can share it with another device at any time.

What's included in the free plan, and what does Pro unlock?

Yard Hopper already includes GPS route optimization, before/after photo proof, and equipment service tracking — completely free. What's capped on the free plan is 5 jobs, 5 customers, 25 invoices, and 1 recurring visit, and Reports requires Pro. Upgrading to Yard Hopper Pro removes all of those limits and adds Cloud sync across your devices.

Is my data private?

By default, everything stays on your device. If you turn on Cloud sync, your data is also stored with our sync provider so your devices can share it. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
